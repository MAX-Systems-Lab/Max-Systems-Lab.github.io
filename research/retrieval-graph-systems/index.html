Systems for Large-Scale Retrieval & Graph Analytics

Hardware/software co-design for retrieval-augmented generation, vector search, and graph processing at scale.

Retrieval-augmented generation, recommendation, genome read mapping, and graph analytics all depend on moving and searching massive irregular data quickly. Building on our work on GPU graph processing, we co-design algorithms, data representations, and hardware for scalable retrieval and graph systems.

Open questions

  • How should retrieval pipelines (indexing, vector search, generation) be co-designed with hardware to meet latency and throughput targets?
  • Can we model the end-to-end performance of RAG serving systems before deploying them?
  • How do large-scale graph and table representations connect to applications such as recommendation and bioinformatics?

Current projects

RAG serving optimization

End-to-end performance modeling and optimization of retrieval-augmented generation pipelines.

Vector search and ANNS indexing

Fast graph-based index construction and billion-scale vector search on modern CPUs and GPUs.

Graph and temporal-graph engines

GPU graph sampling, random walks, triangle counting, and subgraph matching (C-SAW, TEA, TRUST, H-index).

Retrieval for program traces

Applying retrieval systems to large collections of program and performance traces.
