MAX Systems Lab · University of South Florida

Machine learning for faster, explainable, and more efficient computer systems.

We build learned models that predict, explain, and optimize the performance of computer systems: from CPU and GPU microarchitecture, to compilers and programs, to large heterogeneous and AI systems. Our goal is to replace slow, opaque simulation and hand-tuned heuristics with models that are fast, accurate, and tell architects why a design behaves the way it does.
