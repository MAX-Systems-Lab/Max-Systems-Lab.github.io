People

The researchers of MAX Systems Lab.

Principal Investigator

Santosh Pandey

Santosh leads MAX Systems Lab at the University of South Florida. His research uses machine learning to model and optimize computer systems, spanning deep-learning-based microarchitecture simulation (SimNet, TAO), CPU performance modeling (Concorde, with Google), and GPU-accelerated graph and ML systems. His work on Concorde was selected as an IEEE Micro Top Pick (2026), recognizing one of the most significant computer architecture papers of the year.
